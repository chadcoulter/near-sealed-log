How it works

Every sealed entry is anchored with SUM, LINEAR, and PROD checksums mod 231, computed over the action data. A single-character change to the data breaks at least 3 of the 6 verification layers, making tampering publicly detectable.

This contract is the core primitive for our NEAR Hackathon entry: primary track open (inheritance account via Chain Signatures + sealed release rules), secondary track privacy (private expense auditor with TEE attestation + sealed receipts).

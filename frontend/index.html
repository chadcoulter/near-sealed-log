Verification method

Five byte-folded gates produce SUM, LINEAR and PROD checksums modulo 231. These checksums are deterministic, not cryptographic collision-resistant hashes. A matching checksum is an integrity check, not proof of provenance or authenticity.

All reads use NEAR JSON-RPC query/call_function. The browser must permit requests to the configured public RPC endpoint.
